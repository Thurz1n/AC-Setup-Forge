## 🧭 Overview — A Pit Wall in Your Pocket

Imagine a race engineer who never sleeps, never forgets a corner, and never loses patience while you lap the Nordschleife for the two-hundredth time. **AC-Trainer-Vault** is exactly that presence — a companion built around the rhythm of Assetto Corsa, tuned for Windows 10 and Windows 11 systems heading into the 2026 season.

Where most utilities dump raw telemetry at you and walk away, AC-Trainer-Vault reads the way a human coach reads: it watches your braking points, notices when you're overcooking entry speed, and quietly suggests the setup nudge that makes the next lap feel inevitable rather than forced. It's the difference between a toolbox and a teammate.

The **AC-Trainer-Vault** project began as a single notebook of setup notes scribbled between sessions. It grew into a structured assistant for drivers who want their garage philosophy to evolve alongside their muscle memory — and it continues to grow with every release cycle.

> "A setup isn't a magic number. It's a conversation between the car and the driver. This assistant just makes sure both sides are heard."

## 🎯 What This Assistant Actually Does

AC-Trainer-Vault is not a single button. It's a layered workshop of capabilities designed to sit quietly beside your sim rig and speak up only when it has something worth saying.

**Setup Intelligence**
- Analyzes suspension, damping, ARB, and differential balance relative to each circuit's character.
- Tracks how a setup feels across tyre compounds and fuel loads rather than treating one lap as gospel.
- Suggests incremental changes instead of dramatic swings, so you always know what caused what.

**Session Journaling**
- Logs every practice, qualifying, and race stint in a structured, searchable format.
- Builds a personal history of what worked, where, and under what conditions.
- Lets you revisit a setup from three months ago and understand *why* it felt fast.

**Driver Feedback Coach**
- Translates vague sensations ("it feels floaty on exit") into terminology you can act on.
- Offers vocabulary prompts that help you describe understeer, snap oversteer, and traction loss with precision.
- Grows a personal "feedback fingerprint" so recommendations become more personal over time.

**Reference Overlays**
- Provides corner-by-corner reference notes you can pin to your screen layout.
- Highlights braking zones, apex speeds, and gear recommendations without clutter.
- Works across a wide catalogue of community-favourite circuits.

**Season Tracker**
- Keeps a lightweight record of progress across a season, from winter testing to season finale.
- Visualizes consistency trends rather than just peak lap times.
- Encourages the long game — the discipline that separates quick drivers from fast ones.

## 🖼️ A Closer Look at the Experience

Picture this: it's late evening, the room is dim, and you're twenty laps into a long-run test at Spa. Most tools would be flashing numbers at you. AC-Trainer-Vault instead whispers a single sentence: *"Rear ride height feels one click high on this fuel load — try a small drop and repeat three laps."* You do. The car settles. You understand why.

That's the philosophy woven through every screen. The assistant is deliberately restrained. It respects your focus. It surfaces insights at the right moment and stays silent otherwise.

The layout itself is built around three zones:

1. **The Glance Zone** — Critical status indicators, always visible, never demanding.
2. **The Study Zone** — Trends, comparisons, and historical context when you have time to dig.
3. **The Workshop Zone** — The place where you tune, save, and evolve setups.

Together they form a cockpit-within-a-cockpit — a dedicated mental space for reflection between stints.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Beginning your journey with AC-Trainer-Vault is meant to feel like arriving at a well-organised garage, not like assembling furniture.

1. **Review the prerequisites.** A Windows 10 or Windows 11 machine with a reasonably modern GPU and a stable internet connection is all that's conceptually required.
2. **Consult the documentation folder.** The docs describe the conceptual model — sessions, setups, presets — before touching any interface.
3. **Familiarise yourself with the interface tour.** A short guided walkthrough exists inside the app.
4. **Create your first session profile.** This is your identity within the assistant — your driving history starts here.
5. **Record a baseline lap.** Even a messy one. Baselines are not about speed; they're about reference.
6. **Begin the feedback loop.** Use the coaching prompts, adjust one variable at a time, and log what you feel.
7. **Review weekly.** The season tracker rewards consistency over heroics.

For those migrating from an older workflow, the import tools are gentle: your previous journals and notes can be brought forward without retyping anything.

## 🧪 Recommended Workflow for New Drivers

If you're new to sim racing and intimidated by the depth of setup vocabulary, you are precisely the driver this project was built for.

Start by driving. Don't tune anything for your first three sessions. Let the assistant watch. Let it build a picture of how you naturally drive — where you're brave, where you're cautious, where you hesitate. Only after that baseline should you touch a single slider.

Then move in small steps:

- Change one aspect of the car.
- Log how it felt in your own words.
- Repeat the same stint conditions.
- Compare notes with the assistant's observation.
- Decide together whether to keep the change.

This rhythm — small step, honest note, patient comparison — is how real engineering happens. It's also how confidence is built.

## 🛎️ Support & Community

Support runs around the clock. If a panel misbehaves, a translation reads oddly, or a recommendation seems off-base, we want to know. Feedback of all sizes is a gift.

When reporting an issue, include:

- Your Windows version (10 or 11).
- A short description of what you expected versus what happened.
- The session context if relevant (track, car class, session type).
- Any notes from your latest session journal that might explain the state.

For non-urgent suggestions, feature ideas, or philosophical disagreements about setup approach, use the discussion area rather than the issue tracker. Ideas deserve a slower, more thoughtful channel.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data Handling

Your session journals, setup notes, and personal feedback stay on your machine. AC-Trainer-Vault is built around the principle that driving data is personal data. Nothing is uploaded by default, and future cloud features (if any) will always be opt-in, clearly explained, and reversible.

Anonymised, aggregate statistics may be used solely to improve the recommendation engine, and only when explicitly enabled.
